Exact Lower-Order Certiﬁcates and a Search for Log-Concave
Violations of Gaussian Complete Monotonicity
Computational research record
November 17, 2026 — provisional during computation
Abstract
We describe reduced entropy-derivative formulas, constrained density families, and a mixed-
precision computational search at orders six, seven, and eight. The formulas are independently
checked against high-precision heat-convolution ﬁnite diﬀerences. Rigorous ﬁnite-interval in-
tegration is combined with analytic tail bounds and global convexity estimates. Exact ratio-
nal sum-of-squares identities additionally yield the sharp Gaussian lower bounds at orders six
through eight under the stated analytic hypotheses. A further exact certiﬁcate establishes the
nonnegative sign at order nine. This document is an ongoing-results record: an explicit order-17
witness is certiﬁed, while the exact minimal order remains unresolved.

The target is the smallest m ≥6 admitting a smooth positive probability density with V = −log p,

order-six witness would establish minimality. An order-seven or order-eight witness alone would
establish only an upper bound.

Numerical validation and rigorous certiﬁcates
For p(x) = ϕ(x)(1 + sin x/10), exact Gaussian conditioning gives its heat convolution. At 100
decimal digits, 160-node Gauss–Hermite quadrature and a 24-point positive-time ﬁnite diﬀerence
give relative diﬀerences 1.12 · 10−22, 1.92 · 10−22, and 1.17 · 10−19 at orders six, seven, and eight. A
separate 96-to-160-node convergence check has maximum relative change below 2.86 · 10−13. These
are numerical checks, not interval proofs.
Certiﬁcation uses exact rational or decimal parameters, 200-digit Arb arithmetic, and complex
analytic integration on [−L, L]. The integrands are entire or meromorphic; poles produce nonﬁnite
balls and are rejected by the integration procedure. A uniform bound on |Fm|, obtained from its
absolute coeﬃcients and uniform log-derivative bounds, multiplies an analytic tail estimate. For a
multiplier g with |g| ≤M,
