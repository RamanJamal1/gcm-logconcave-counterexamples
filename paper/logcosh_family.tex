\section{An exact exclusion for a family with exponential tails}
A supplementary calculation treats the entire two-parameter family
\[
 p_{\alpha,\beta}(x)=Z_{\alpha,\beta}^{-1}
 e^{\beta x}\cosh(x)^{-\alpha},\qquad \alpha>0,\quad |\beta|<\alpha.
\]
Its potential has $V''=\alpha\operatorname{sech}^2x>0$ everywhere,
although it has no uniform positive curvature lower bound. All higher
log derivatives are bounded, and exponential tails justify the displayed
entropy differentiation and integration by parts.
\begin{proposition}[Tilted hyperbolic-secant family]
For this family, $S_m(p_{\alpha,\beta})>0$ for every $2\le m\le17$.
This assertion concerns this family only and does not raise the universal
lower endpoint of the bracket for $m^*$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $T=\tanh X$. Then $(1+T)/2$ has the beta distribution with parameters
$(\alpha+\beta)/2$ and $(\alpha-\beta)/2$. Hence, writing $M_k=\mathbb E T^k$,
\[
 M_0=1,\quad M_1=\frac\beta\alpha,\qquad
 M_{k+1}=\frac{\beta M_k+kM_{k-1}}{\alpha+k}.
\]
The score derivatives of order at least two are $\alpha$ times polynomials
in $T$, obtained by applying $(1-T^2)\partial_T$ repeatedly to $-T$.
Substitute these into the exact entropy recurrence. After multiplication
by $(\alpha)_{2m}=\prod_{j=0}^{2m-1}(\alpha+j)$ and the substitution
$s=1-\beta^2/\alpha^2\in(0,1]$, the result is a polynomial
\[
 (\alpha)_{2m}S_m
 =\sum_i\alpha^i\sum_{k=0}^{n_i}
 b_{m,i,k}\binom{n_i}{k}s^k(1-s)^{n_i-k}.
\]
The exact rational arrays in
\path{certs/logcosh_family_coefficients.json} have $b_{m,i,k}\ge0$,
and for each $m$ at least one endpoint coefficient $b_{m,i,n_i}>0$.
That term is strictly positive whenever $\alpha>0$ and $s>0$.
The separate verifier reconstructs the entropy polynomial independently,
computes the expectations using beta raw moments and the binomial theorem,
and checks every Bernstein identity and coefficient sign exactly.
Its record is \path{certs/independent_logcosh_family.json}.
\end{proof}
