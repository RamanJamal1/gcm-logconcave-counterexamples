# Process records of the AI-driven research runs

These files are the records of the automated research runs that produced the certificates and counterexamples: the long-run prompt, run logs, status files, progress dashboards, morning briefings and handoff notes. They are kept for transparency about how the work was done, as described in the paper's statement on the use of AI.

They are not part of any proof. The results stand or fall with the verifiers and certificates in `src/` and `certs/`, and the paper in `paper/`. The working directories of the individual research tracks are under `tracks/`.

The files are unedited except that a GitHub account name was replaced by `[redacted-github-account]`. Wording such as "running" or "in progress" is historical.
